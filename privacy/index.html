Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

mayanklovesrichika.life is our little world: a personal website run by Richika & Mayank for us, our family and our friends. It isn't a business, and we never sell, rent or share your information.

What we collect

  • If we've shared a personal link with you (for example an invitation to one of our events): your household's name and the people we added, plus anything you tell us, such as replies, food preferences, travel plans or notes.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account ID, only to recognise you next time. We don't access your contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.
  • Anything you choose to add, such as photos or messages, if and when the site offers that.
  • Cookies: one essential sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in. No advertising cookies.
  • Basic usage analytics: anonymous page views, to see whether the site works. Never your name, email or phone number.

How we use it

Only to show you what we've shared with you, to plan things together (like who's coming to an event) and to stay in touch.

Who can see it

Everything is private by default. Your details are kept in a private database hosted by Railway, and only the two of us can see them. Your personal link or sign-in gives you access to your own details and to what we've chosen to share with you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete anything we hold about you at any time. Just write to mayankgupta690@gmail.com. We delete details we no longer need, such as replies to an event, within a year after it.
